Privacy Policy

jenjmadison.com  ·  Jen J. Madison, Costume Designer
Last updated 30 August 2026

This site is a personal portfolio for the costume design work of Jen J. Madison. It is a small static website: there are no accounts, no logins, no comment sections, and no forms that collect your information.

What this website collects

No personal information is requested or stored by this site. Nothing you do here is tied to your identity.

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which records ordinary web-server information such as IP addresses in order to deliver pages and protect against abuse. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Analytics. The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It is cookieless, it does not fingerprint visitors, and it reports only aggregate figures — how many people viewed a page, not who they were. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers to fetch them.

No cookies are set by this site. No data is sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

The research application

Separately from this website, the site owner runs a professional research application for her own use. It keeps notes on the productions she is designing — research, sourcing, schedules and decisions — so that the information is in one place across a season.

This application is not offered to the public and has no users other than the site owner. It is described here because it authenticates against Google APIs, and Google requires a published privacy policy for any application that does so.

  • Whose data it touches. Only the site owner's own Google account. It reads and writes documents, spreadsheets and files in her own Google Drive, using credentials she granted to herself.
  • Where the data goes. Content retrieved from Google is used solely to provide features to the site owner, and is stored on private infrastructure controlled by her. It is not published, sold, transferred, or used for advertising, and it is not used to train generalized artificial-intelligence models.
  • Access by people. No human other than the site owner and the individual who maintains that infrastructure on her behalf reads this data, except where required by law.
  • Revoking access. The Google authorization can be withdrawn at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops all access.

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
